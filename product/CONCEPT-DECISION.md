## Retain

A folded paper fortune teller with eight conventional compartments reveals an **impossible ninth fold**. The user's actual colour and number selection reappears in a tiny fortune teller within. Manipulating the miniature forces the exterior object to physically change. Horror is *spatial recursion with visible causal inversion*.

The selected mark must not be a prerecorded universal image; it must be bound to actual player choice. The nested input must actively change a State Machine / outer physical pose, not merely replay a detached animation. Authentic handmade paper craft is a core scoring surface.

## Reject or deprioritize

- Generic haunted UI, fog/glitch and common photodarkroom tricks.
- Procedural pumpkin carving (FACE VALUE) unless a real freehand->shape->expressive rig exists; adjacent to challenge's Gourdgeous designer.
- False 3D or fake infinite geometry claims; 2.5D vector/mesh composition is acceptable if independently perceived.
- The Great Reveal as passive 15s cutscene with no meaningful choices.
- Reusing the puppet/shadow agency reversal from ONE FRAME LATE.

## Alternative

**LAST IMPRESSION** — *letterpress printing press*, explicitly not the photographic/darkroom proposals; only if the paper-fold technical spike fails and the letterpress can be built/presented before deadline.

## Reopen decision rule

Evaluate the **whole work**, not just the fold: (a) visual/editorial artistic identity, (b) a protagonist's motive with player consequence, (c) connected stage/world reactions to choice, (d) earned horror climax, (e) alternative ending/replay, (f) Rive-native artistic implementation and Web performance, (g) 5-second comprehension from a stranger.

The 90–180min fold kill spike is necessary to avoid waste but explicitly not sufficient for promotion. Before final lock, test whether the proposed setting and characters feel sufficiently differentiated from Halloween Hotel, DOMOVOI, Trick or Room and paper-village Night Post, rather than imitating their feature count. Keep the option to change the setting if concept review disproves it.

## Decision gates

Do not Concept Lock until source-verified originality, Rive technical feasibility, Editor and browser behavior, negative/recovery path and at least a bounded cold-player comprehension test support the idea. The deadline is a scope constraint, not proof.
