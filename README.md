# THE NINTH FOLD

**Rive Halloween Challenge 2026 — independent second-entry candidate.**

A handmade paper fortune teller begins with eight ordinary predictions. When the viewer's own colour and number selection opens a forbidden ninth fold, the miniature paper world inside remembers that choice. A gesture inside the miniature makes the *outer* paper fold move.

> The strange thing isn't that the fortune is haunted. The strange thing is that the smaller paper object can fold the larger one.
